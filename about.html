About Me

Who am I?

My name is Jeremy Millin. I was born on August 18th, 2009, and I was born and raised in New York City. As a person, I would describe myself as hardworking, passionate, creative, and someone who always looks to lift others up. I am organized, dependable, and am a great public speaker. My strongest skill in the workspace is the skill of communicating my ideas and connecting with others in a charismatic way. I am very vocal, and always capable of delivering a full fledged speech off the top of my head on the spot if the time ever comes (it has came a couple times in my life).

Acedemic Background

I have been attending The Browning School since I was in 5th grade. Since then, I have been an active member of the Browning community, and established my place as a hard working individual who is passionate about learning, growning, and being the best version of myself. I love connecting with those in my community, and always say hello to students and faculty as I walk past them in the hallways.
