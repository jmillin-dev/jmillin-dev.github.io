Music Production

My recreation of the synth from Take on Me by Aha using Serum

My mom’s absolute favorite song is Take On Me by Aha. While learning how to use Serum 2, I wanted to surprise her by recreating Aha’s iconic synth melody. The process took a lot of trial and error, but I finally achieved the correct sound by using the right combination of Envelopes, LFOs, and FX.

Chemistry

I enjoy doing chemistry experiments, and have recently done a Copper (II) Sulfate and Sodium Hydroxide reaction with my friend Jeri

I always loved chemistry class. Creating Lewis Dot Diagrams, finding the molarity of solutions, and balancing redox reactions always felt more like a fun puzzle than hard work. So when I had the opportunity to explore solution reactions in depth in my final project, I was very excited. With my partner Jeri, we chose to do a copper (II) sulfate solution and sodium hydroxide double replacement reaction to produce a blue copper (II) hydroxide precipitate due to our shared love of Breaking Bad. We wanted to make a blue precipitate to match Heisenberg’s signature product, as an homage to our favorite show. While we had a silly motive, we took the project very seriously, and actually managed to write an entire research paper.

Basketball

I love watching basketball, and went to a Euroleague game on my exchange program

I love basketball. My whole life, I have been a huge Knicks fan. While my fandom originally started as a small hobby, I ended up getting more into it as I grew older. I became a basketball historian of sorts, being able to list (almost) every NBA Champion off the top of my head. I also got very into advanced stats, looking at all the metrics to calculate the best basketball players and teams statistically (PER, VORP, BPM, TS%, etc.). I even read a very interesting book, called The Midrange Theory, by Seth Partnow, where I learned how the game of basketball evolved into a sport of data and analytics, where coaches and analysts have calculated every aspect of the game to find the statistically best strategies, such as spacing, shot selection, team building, scouting, and much more. Not only am I into the statistics aspect of the game, I always love watching games, and experiencing the fan culture. One of the most unique basketball experiences I have participated in was during my exchange program, where I went to a Barcelona basketball game. While I am from New York, and know the craziness and passion of New York Knicks fans, it was still very interesting to see Spanish fan culture in a basketball setting. Instead of chanting, fans were singing, and instead of arena sound effects, fans brought in their own drums. You could feel the beat of the drums deep in your heart while the players were fighting for their lives on the court. It ended up being a very close game, and fortunately, Barcelona scraped out with the win. This game was a life changing experience that I will never forget.
